# Focus Guard Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

Focus Guard is a browser extension that interrupts short-video feeds with small puzzles and helps you focus with a timer, tasks and progress tracking. This policy explains what data it handles.

## Summary

**Focus Guard does not collect, sell, share or transmit any personal data.** It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. Everything it stores stays in your browser on your device.

## What is stored, and where

Focus Guard saves the following in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). This storage is private to the extension and is never sent anywhere.

| Data | Why |
|------|-----|
| Your settings (timer lengths, daily goal, puzzle types, which sites the guard runs on) | To remember your choices |
| Tasks and quick todos you type in | To show your task list |
| Timer state | To keep the timer running when the popup is closed |
| Counts of short videos watched per site, time on those feeds, puzzles solved | To show your stats |
| A day-by-day activity log (last 60 days) and lifetime totals | For streaks, the focus garden, weekly charts and levels |

Focus Guard does **not** record which videos you watch, their titles or authors, your browsing history, or anything you type into websites. On supported sites it only notices that the video on screen has changed, so it can count it.

## Website access

- **YouTube** (`youtube.com`) access is part of the extension, so it can count YouTube Shorts and show puzzles.
- **Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Snapchat** access is **optional**. Your browser asks for your permission only when you switch a site on in Focus Guard, and switching it off removes that access.

On these sites the extension only reads the page address and on-screen video elements to detect a new video, and shows its own puzzle overlay. It does not read or change your messages, posts, account or any other page content.

## Network requests

Focus Guard makes no network requests. Its fonts and images are bundled inside the extension. The only internal file read is the extension's own `manifest.json`, to tell you when a reload is needed after an update.

## Removing your data

Uninstall the extension to delete all of its data. You can also clear stats with **Reset stats** in the Guard tab and delete tasks or todos individually.

## Children

Focus Guard does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
