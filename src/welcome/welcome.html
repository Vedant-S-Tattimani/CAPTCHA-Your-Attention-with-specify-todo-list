Focus Guard

Plan your work, focus in short sprints, grow a garden while you do it, and get a gentle nudge when YouTube Shorts start eating your evening.

Get started in 3 steps

  1. 1
    Pin the extension. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Focus Guard so the timer badge is always visible.
  2. 2
    Add a task and press 🎯. That's what you're focusing on. It shows on the timer and on every Shorts puzzle.
  3. 3
    Start a 25-minute session. Or press Alt+Shift+F on any page. Each finished session grows today's plant.
